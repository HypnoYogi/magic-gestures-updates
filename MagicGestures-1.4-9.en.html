Floopi for the Mac now also accepts other common files, for example Word, Excel and PowerPoint files (docx, xlsx, pptx), text, music or zip.

  • The Mac stores them in the destination folder and does not open them by itself.
  • PDFs, as well as photos and videos in common formats (for example JPG, PNG, HEIC, MP4, MOV), open as before.
  • Floopi does not accept files with the extension of a program, script or web page.
  • If the destination folder is on an exFAT or FAT32 drive or on a network drive, Floopi does not accept other files there.
  • The tab and the button for pairing are now called “Phone” and “Pair phone…”.
  • Bug fixes for file name checks.

You can throw other files with Floopi for iPhone 1.4 or later and Floopi for Android 0.7.0 or later, as soon as the respective version is available in the App Store or on Google Play. Until then, each phone app throws as before.
