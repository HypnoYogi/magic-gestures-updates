Floopi for the Mac now accepts several files in one throw, up to 50.

  • Up to three files open one by one, as before.
  • From four files on you hear a sound and a pop-up shows the number of files. Floopi then shows the folder in Finder with the files that arrived selected.
  • Under “Diagnostics”, in “Recent Throws”, a batch appears as one row.

Floopi for iPhone 1.3 or later and Floopi for Android 0.6.0 or later throw several files at once as soon as the respective version is available in the App Store or on Google Play. Until then, each app throws one file at a time.
